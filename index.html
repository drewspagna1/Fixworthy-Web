Repair it, replace it, or live with it?

Every source of advice on this question has a stake in the outcome, retailers want you to replace, repair guides want you to repair. Fixworthy doesn't sell parts or install labor. It answers the question honestly, and shows its work.

Repair yourself
Parts, tools, your time, and the risk of getting it wrong.
Hire a repair
Labor and parts, weighed against the convenience.
Replace
New price minus resale, plus disposal and setup friction.
Live with it
Accept degraded function and defer the cost, a real option.

Describe what's broken in your own words. Fixworthy estimates the missing numbers, labels every guess as a guess, and ranks all four paths with the reasoning behind each, never a single verdict with no explanation. Every slider is yours to move, and the recommendation moves with it.

No stake in the outcome

Fixworthy doesn't sell repair parts, doesn't broker repair jobs, and doesn't take a cut of a replacement purchase. The app works fully offline, no account required to get a verdict. That's not a feature, it's the point: nothing about how the app makes money can lean the answer one way.

Built for real decisions

Starting with the categories where this decision actually bites: appliances, phones and electronics, and everyday gear. Each verdict shows the repair-to-replace ratio, a remaining-useful-life estimate, and what a repeat repair does to the math, plain numbers, plain English, nothing hidden.
